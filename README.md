Exploratory analysis of **9,800 retail orders (2015–2018)** totaling **$2.26M in sales** — identifying top categories, regional gaps, and growth trends to guide business decisions.

![Sales Analysis Dashboard](sales_analysis.png)

## 🎯 Business Question
Where does the revenue come from, how is it growing, and where are the untapped opportunities?

## 🔍 Key Insights
- **$2.26M total sales**, led by **Technology ($827K)**.
- **~50% growth in four years** — from **$480K (2015)** to **$722K (2018)**, after a dip in 2016.
- **Geographic concentration:** California and New York generate **~33%** of all sales.
- **Consumer segment** drives **51%** of revenue.
- **South is the weakest region** at **17.2%** of sales.

## 🗄️ SQL Analysis
Ten business questions answered in SQL (SQLite) — CTEs, window functions (`LAG`, `RANK`), subqueries, and cohort retention.
Queries: [`sql/queries.sql`](sql/queries.sql) · Results: [`sql/RESULTS.md`](sql/RESULTS.md)

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Year-over-year growth | **-4.3%** (2016) → **+30.6%** (2017) → **+20.3%** (2018) |
| Average order value | **$459.48** across 4,922 orders and 793 customers |
| Seasonality | **Sep, Nov, Dec** generate **43%** of annual sales |
| Customer retention | **99.5%** of 2015 customers bought again in a later year |
| Top sub-categories | **Phones** ($328K) and **Chairs** ($323K) |
| Shipping speed | Standard Class averages **5.0 days** vs **2.2** for First Class |

```sql
-- Year-over-year growth with a window function
WITH yearly AS (
    SELECT year, SUM(sales) AS sales FROM orders GROUP BY year
)
SELECT year,
       ROUND(sales, 2) AS sales,
       ROUND(100.0 * (sales - LAG(sales) OVER (ORDER BY year))
             / LAG(sales) OVER (ORDER BY year), 1) AS yoy_growth_pct
FROM yearly
ORDER BY year;
```

## 💡 Recommendations
1. **Grow the South region** with targeted marketing and promotions.
2. **Reduce dependence on California and New York** by expanding in other states.
3. **Prioritize Technology** — the highest-revenue category.
4. **Develop the Corporate and Home Office segments** to diversify revenue.
5. **Plan inventory and campaigns for Q4** — September to December drive the bulk of annual sales.

## 🛠️ Process
1. **Import** — 9,800 records × 18 columns
2. **Cleaning** — handled missing values, parsed dates, removed duplicates, engineered Year/Month features
3. **Analysis** — sales by category, region, state, segment, and time
4. **Visualization** — four-panel KPI dashboard
5. **SQL** — ten business questions answered with SQLite queries
